Industrial refrigeration

Data from any brand, in one piece of software.

Whatever the device or brand, Frigora makes sense of the data in the field. It reads, reports, and adjusts variables remotely.

See the systems Who it's for

01 — Approach

Separate monitoring setups, one site.

Industrial refrigeration already has many monitoring systems. Each brand runs its own. Software that follows those setups together has been missing from the field.

Frigora is that layer. When the device or brand changes, it still defines the data, reads it the same way, and reaches the variable remotely.

02 — Capability

Four jobs the software does.

  1. 01

    Interpretation

    It defines incoming data by brand and device. It is not locked to one model family.

  2. 02

    Reading

    Measurement and status are brought into one place, instead of left on separate screens.

  3. 03

    Reporting

    Collected data becomes a record that can be followed. The value does not disappear across scattered systems.

  4. 04

    Remote adjustment

    Variables can be reached without a trip to the site each time. Adjustment sits beside reading.

Work that would take costly software and hardware is offered through simpler, more effective software.

03 — Systems

The brand changes. The layer stays.

Each installation arrives with its own monitoring setup. Those setups do not follow one another. Frigora does not replace the device in place; it gathers the data in one piece of software.

01

Separate monitoring

02

One plant

A cold room, a display case, and a warehouse stand side by side. Monitoring still stays separate on each device.

03

One piece of software

Frigora reads this data, reports it, and reaches the variable remotely. The brand does not change the screen.

Controllers arrive with different numbers of inputs and outputs. Frigora is not defined by one input-output count.

04 — Who it's for

Sales is built around three groups.

Refrigeration manufacturers

Manufacturers who want monitoring of the plant they built gathered in one piece of software, even when brands are mixed.

Technical service firms

Teams that look after devices from different brands. Reading and remote adjustment stay in one place.

Warehouse, quality, and R&D

The end-user side of the plant. Teams that watch the climate control, keep records, and look at a variable when needed.

05 — Scale

The size of a conditioned space changes.

The same kind of installation is built at very different volumes. A milk cabinet in a store display and a large product warehouse are examples of that range. Scope is not defined by those two sizes; similar installations exist wherever a space is climate-controlled.
